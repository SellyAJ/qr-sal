QR Sal.

Your files stay on your device.

QR Sal decodes selected files in your browser using a local Web Worker. There is no upload endpoint, AI service, API key, account, analytics script, or advertising.

What gets loaded

The static host serves the page, JavaScript, fonts and a synthetic QR sample. PDF.js and its support files load from the same host when you open a PDF. The host can receive ordinary request metadata such as your IP address; QR Sal does not send it the files you select or their decoded text. See GitHub’s privacy statement for hosting details.

Results and exports

Files and results are held in memory. Clear resets the current document; closing the page releases its session. Nothing is intentionally stored in local storage or IndexedDB. Copy and Export JSON run only when you choose them. Exported results include decoded text, page numbers and geometry. If you enable diagnostic tracing, the export can also include source fragments. Inspect exports before sharing them.

Links inside QR codes

Decoded content is displayed as text. QR Sal does not open decoded links automatically or execute decoded markup. Decoding does not authenticate the sender or verify link safety.
